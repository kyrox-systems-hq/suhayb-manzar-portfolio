Where Lylo really started

Not with a trend.
With a watch left behind.

When Tanner’s grandfather passed away, he left him his watch. Tanner wore it through the years when the work was real but the results were not yet visible.

Lylo turns that private reminder into something another person can carry: respect the time, trust the work and keep going before there is proof.
