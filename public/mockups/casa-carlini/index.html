About the author

Abdalhadi Alijla, PhD

Alijla is a Palestinian-Swedish political scientist, author and advocate for social justice. He is the author of Trust in Divided Societies, co-editor of Rebel Governance in the Middle East, and a senior fellow at the Arab Reform Initiative.

Fearful in Gaza is his reckoning, his tribute and his call to remember.
