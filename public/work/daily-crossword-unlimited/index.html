Daily Crossword Unlimited

Turning a technically complex game, content engine and IP library into a clear acquisition proposition with working proof behind every claim.

Context

Three products needed to feel like one investable asset.

Daily Crossword Unlimited includes a published cross-platform game, an automated Python puzzle-generation pipeline and a library of more than 2,000 validated puzzles. Each component has standalone value, but a buyer needed to understand why the complete package mattered commercially.

The website therefore had to do more than describe features. It needed to help a potential acquirer understand the content bottleneck, see the working product and recognise the commercial routes available after acquisition.

Challenge

Technical depth can easily obscure the buying decision.

The project contains algorithms, LLM-generated clue workflows, cloud infrastructure, mobile integrations, quality controls and a large content library. Presenting all of that at once would have created a dense technical catalogue rather than a commercial proposition.

The central challenge was to preserve the proof while simplifying the narrative: a production-ready game that can generate its own content.

Approach

Lead with the business advantage, then earn the technical detail.

Structure around buyer questions

The information architecture moves from the complete asset to the working product, commercial value, buyer fit and next steps. This mirrors the questions a studio, publisher or portfolio buyer is likely to ask.

Make proof visible

The site surfaces the live app, real game screens, generator output, a narrated demonstration and the technology behind the pipeline. Claims about scale and automation are supported by a working system rather than decorative copy.

Keep the acquisition route direct

The calls to action consistently invite a qualified buyer to discuss the asset. Secondary content supports that decision instead of competing with it.
