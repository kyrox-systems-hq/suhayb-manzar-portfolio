# Stage 5: Current Cold-Email Theory Review

Serial: WEBLEADS-STAGE5-20260808-002

## Objective

Before writing any prospect email, research the current evidence on highly personalised cold B2B email and produce one campaign standard.

Read:

- current authoritative and reputable research
- `outreach/config.json`
- `outreach/COLD_EMAIL_BASELINE.md`

Write:

- `outreach/campaigns/<week>/05-email-standard.md`

Do not draft the 25 prospect sequences in this stage.

## Freshness requirement

This review is a weekly campaign input, not permanent boilerplate.

At the top of `05-email-standard.md`, include:

```text
Reviewed: YYYY-MM-DD
Review-ID: WEBLEADS-EMAIL-<week>-<unique-suffix>
```

The review date must be current. Campaign preflight rejects a theory review older than seven days.

Stage 6 must copy both the `Reviewed` date and `Review-ID` into `06-sequences.json`. If the theory standard changes after the sequence file is written, the sequences must be re-confirmed or rewritten before preflight can pass.

## Research priorities

Review current evidence on:

- highly personalised cold email
- opening-line relevance
- subject lines
- message length
- problem and priority framing
- value-led CTAs
- follow-up structure
- number of touches
- timing
- deliverability
- spam-triggering patterns
- mobile readability
- founder and executive outreach
- current legal requirements in the campaign jurisdictions

Prioritise:

- regulator guidance for legal requirements
- primary or large-sample research
- credible sales platforms that publish methodology
- current material over generic blog advice

## Starting hypotheses to test

Do not assume these remain true. Verify them.

- Keep the initial message concise, usually around 50 to 100 words when possible.
- Lead with current account research or a genuine business observation.
- Discuss the prospect's priority or problem before describing our service.
- Avoid buzzwords and unsupported ROI language.
- Offer something concrete, in this campaign the researched live concept.
- Prefer a low-friction interest or implementation CTA over demanding calendar time immediately.
- Follow-ups should add value or a new angle.
- Avoid guilt language such as "I never heard back".
- Avoid empty bubble-ups such as "just following up".
- Maintain three recipient business days between touches because this campaign has explicitly chosen that cadence.

## Campaign Email Standard

The final standard must define:

- initial-email target length
- follow-up target length
- subject-line rules
- opening-line rules
- personalisation evidence threshold
- problem-framing rules
- how the live mock-up should be introduced
- CTA rules
- forbidden phrases and tactics
- follow-up purpose for touches 2 to 5
- compliance footer/unsubscribe requirements by country
- deliverability safeguards
- scheduling rules

Cite the sources used. Use at least two credible current source URLs, and use regulator sources for legal/compliance rules where relevant.

The governing principle is:

**The recipient should feel that one person researched one business and built something specifically for it, not that a mail-merge engine inserted their name into a template.**
