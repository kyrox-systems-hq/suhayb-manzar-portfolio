# Stage 1: Commercial Discovery

Serial: WEBLEADS-STAGE1-20260807-001

## Objective

Create the raw discovery pool for one weekly campaign.

Do not build mock-ups.
Do not draft emails.
Do not conduct deep prospect research.

## Inputs

Read:

- `outreach/config.json`
- `public/mockups/_outreach-ledger.json`
- every existing business represented under `public/mockups/`
- `outreach/campaigns/<week>/campaign.json`

Use BuiltWith as the primary commercial discovery source.

If `BUILTWITH_API_KEY` is available, run:

```bash
npm run outreach:discover -- --week=<week>
```

If a BuiltWith export was supplied, use:

```bash
npm run outreach:discover -- --week=<week> --input=<path>
```

## Commercial filters

Target ecommerce businesses in the US, UK and Canada.

Prefer:

- estimated monthly ecommerce sales of approximately USD 30,000 to USD 300,000
- 2 to 50 employees
- at least USD 100 estimated monthly technology spend
- meaningful ecommerce catalogue
- recent BuiltWith detection or change
- evidence of active analytics, paid media, marketing automation or ecommerce tooling

Revenue and employee figures are estimates. Never present them as verified facts.

## Duplicate rule

A prospect is permanently excluded if any of these match earlier work:

- business name
- trading name
- domain or redirected domain
- parent company
- owner or decision-maker
- contact email
- prior source URL
- substantially identical storefront

Do not recycle rejected prospects.

## Output

Produce approximately 40 to 50 candidates in:

`outreach/campaigns/<week>/01-discovered.json`

Each record must include the discovery fields generated by the CLI plus, where available:

- why it appears commercially active
- initial website-problem signal
- important technologies
- preliminary commercial score

Do not reject a candidate merely because an email has not yet been checked. Email verification is Stage 2.

End after discovery.
