The concept must demonstrate the Stage 3 commercial hypothesis.

Do not redesign an entire website merely to make it prettier.

Use only genuine public business assets and facts.

Never fabricate:

- products
- prices
- discounts
- reviews
- claims
- certifications
- addresses
- delivery promises
- photography
- logos
