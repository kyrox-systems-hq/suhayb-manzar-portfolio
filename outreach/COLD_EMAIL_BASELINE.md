## Message design

Gong's 2026 executive-email analysis reports that reply rates fall sharply above 100 words and that the strongest executive emails are generally 50 to 100 words. It recommends focusing on the prospect's priority rather than seller buzzwords and using an offer of value rather than immediately demanding a meeting.

Source:
https://www.gong.io/blog/do-execs-really-reply-to-cold-email-here-s-what-the-data-says

Gong's analysis of more than 28 million cold emails, published in 2025 and updated in 2026, reports that pitching can reduce reply rates substantially, that shorter emails perform better, and that the strongest reply rates in that analysis came from messages of 100 words or fewer and roughly three to four sentences.

Source:
https://www.gong.io/blog/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says

Outreach recommends beginning personalised manual prospecting emails with current, relevant account research, then connecting it to risk or pain, the solution, proof and a CTA. Its guidance recommends conversational language, fewer than 150 words, and region-aware sequence timing.

Source:
https://support.outreach.io/support/solutions/articles/159000425763-outreach-s-best-practices-for-personalizing-emails

## Follow-ups

Gong's analysis of 304,174 follow-up prospecting emails distinguishes concise from merely short. Its findings support follow-ups that contain useful prospect-specific value rather than empty bubble-ups.

Source:
https://www.gong.io/blog/7-tips-for-writing-the-perfect-follow-up-sales-email-according-to-science

Gong's broader cold-email research advises against guilt-based language such as telling the buyer that they never replied, and supports interest-led CTAs rather than forcing a meeting request.

Source:
https://www.gong.io/blog/cold-email-stats

For this campaign, the user-selected cadence is fixed at three recipient business days between each touch.

## Compliance baseline

### United States

CAN-SPAM applies to commercial B2B email as well as consumer email. Commercial messages must use accurate headers and subjects, identify the commercial nature of the message as required, include a valid postal address, provide a clear opt-out mechanism and honour opt-outs within the required period.

Source:
https://search.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

### United Kingdom

ICO guidance states that PECR's electronic-mail marketing rule does not apply to corporate subscribers such as companies and LLPs in the same way it applies to individual subscribers. Sole traders and certain partnerships receive stronger protection. Where personal data is used, UK GDPR still applies and a lawful basis is required.

Source:
https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/direct-marketing-and-privacy-and-electronic-communications/business-to-business-marketing/

### Canada

CRTC guidance states that CASL can recognise implied consent through conspicuous publication only where the address was conspicuously published, no statement says unsolicited commercial messages are unwanted, and the message is relevant to the recipient's business role, functions or duties. The sender bears the burden of proving the consent basis.

Source:
https://crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/guide.htm

### Australia

ACMA guidance states that commercial electronic messages require consent. Inferred consent is limited and cannot be assumed simply because contact details are public. Australia is therefore excluded by default from this workflow unless a specific consent basis is verified.

Source:
https://www.acma.gov.au/avoid-sending-spam

## Campaign-specific standard

Until Stage 5 finds better current evidence, use these defaults:

- Touch 1: normally 50 to 100 words, never above the configured 110-word hard limit.
- Touches 2 to 5: normally 35 to 80 words, never above the configured 90-word hard limit.
- Subject: short, factual and non-promotional.
- Opening: a real observation or current commercial signal specific to the business.
- Problem: explain the website issue without pretending to know internal conversion data.
- Offer: the already-built live concept is the concrete value.
- CTA: ask whether they want the demonstrated change implemented, rather than forcing a meeting.
- Follow-ups: each must introduce a different useful angle.
- Never use guilt, fake familiarity, generic compliments or unsupported ROI claims.
- Do not use ordinary scheduled send if it cannot stop remaining messages after a reply.
