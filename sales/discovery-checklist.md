# Website discovery checklist

Serial: WEB-DISC-20260726-001

1. What does the business sell?
2. Who is the priority buyer?
3. What should the visitor do next?
4. What currently exists?
5. What is not working?
6. Which pages are essential at launch?
7. What proof can be shown?
8. Is the copy already approved?
9. Which assets are available?
10. What deadline is driving the project?
11. Who approves the work?
12. Which domain and hosting accounts are involved?
