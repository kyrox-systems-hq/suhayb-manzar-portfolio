# Outreach playbook

Serial: WEB-SALES-20260726-001

## Qualification gate

Only contact a lead when the individual post is accessible, the date is verified, the buyer appears genuine and the need is less than seven days old. Prioritise posts from the previous 72 hours.

Score each lead out of ten:

- Urgency: 0 to 3
- Commercial fit: 0 to 3
- Budget evidence: 0 to 2
- Freshness: 0 to 1
- Clear contact route: 0 to 1

Contact leads scoring seven or above first.

## Public response pattern

1. Refer to the exact problem they described.
2. Give one useful observation before pitching.
3. Mention one relevant project as proof.
4. Offer a small next step, usually a quick inspection of the current site or brief.

## Private message pattern

Keep the message personal and short. Do not repeat the public comment. State what you noticed, what you would inspect first and what information you need to give them a fixed scope.

## Follow-up rule

Send one follow-up after two working days when the buyer has shown interest or asked for information. Do not repeatedly chase a cold lead.

## Platform discipline

Do not mass-post identical messages. Check each community's rules before commenting or sending a private message. The portfolio link supports a relevant answer rather than replacing one.
